Money: the cheap-yen split

Yen near historic lows. Tourists get discount Japan; exporters post record quarters (Nikkei all-time highs: 54,341 in January, 60,000 in April 2026). Households absorb it via import prices on energy and food. Record symbols of the year so far: a ¥510 million bluefin at Toyosu's new-year auction.

Politics: rightward drift, rails intact

LDP under PM Sanae Takaichi (first woman in the job) won a postwar-record 316 of 465 lower-house seats in the February 2026 snap election. Article 9 constitutional revision is openly debated with large counter-protests (tens of thousands, multiple cities). Arms-export ban for lethal weapons lifted April 2026; first postwar National Intelligence Bureau approved July 2026; flag-desecration penalties in force August 2026. Ongoing China-Japan diplomatic crisis since November 2025 (Taiwan statements, dual-use export bans both ways). Assessment: real drift, democratic rails holding, milder than the European/American variants. Revisit every snapshot.
