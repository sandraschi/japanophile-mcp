Learning materials, online tools, and exchange

One main textbook spine plus one SRS/review system beats a shelf of unfinished books. Mix free and paid resources; verify any site still exists before paying annual fees.

Integrated textbooks (classroom-style)

  • Genki I & II — Common in North American universities; clear grammar, audio, workbook. Pair with Genki Kanji Look and Learn.
  • Minna no Nihongo — Japanese-first sentences; needs translation supplement for most English speakers.
  • Marugoto — CEFR-aligned tasks; good for communication-focused paths.
  • Quartet / Tobira / An Integrated Approach — Intermediate bridges after Genki.
  • Shin Kanzen Master — Exam drill series (JLPT-shaped); use after N4 foundation, not as first book.

Kanji-dedicated

  • Kodansha Kanji Learner's Course — Gradual with compounds.
  • Remembering the Kanji (Heisig) — Stories first; add readings separately.
  • WaniKani — Paid SRS web; strong community.

Grammar reference

  • Dictionary of Basic / Intermediate / Advanced Japanese Grammar (Makino) — Shelf essential.
  • Handbook of Japanese Grammar (Tae Kim) — Free web + book.
  • Bunpro — SRS grammar paths tagged to exam levels.

Online audio and courses

  • NHK World Easy Japanese — Free graded lessons + audio.
  • Japanese Pod 101 — Tiered podcasts (quality varies by level).
  • Duolingo / Lingodeer — Habit builders; not sufficient alone for exams.
  • YouTube — 日本語の森, Cure Dolly (legacy), Miku Real Japanese — pick one series, finish it.
  • Anki — Shared decks; prefer sentence cards you edit yourself.

Dictionaries and lookup

  • Jisho.org — Quick JMdict lookup.
  • Weblio / Goo — Monolingual definitions when ready.
  • Yomiwa / Takoboto — Mobile OCR and paste lookup in Japan.
  • This app's kanji tools + Practice games when offline.

Language exchange (speaking output)

Exams do not test speaking — you must schedule it yourself.

  • HelloTalk / Tandem — Text/voice chat with learners; filter by seriousness.
  • italki / Preply — Paid tutors; cheapest way to fix keigo and pronunciation.
  • Meetup / local university clubs — In-person if available.
  • Discord / Reddit r/LearnJapanese — Occasional voice groups; verify safety norms.

Tips: fixed weekly slot; prepare topics; correct register (teineigo default); do not only chat in English.

Reading ladder

  • Graded readers (White Rabbit, Tadoku free corpus).
  • Manga with furigana (よつばと!, Shirokuma Cafe).
  • NHK News Web Easy → mainstream news.
  • Light novels after N3 solid.

Textbook for sequence; Language tabs for maps (especially Keigo and Exams); Practice for volume; Knowledge for society context; MCP Tools when an agent helps your study log or lookup workflow.
